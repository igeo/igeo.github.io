The details

1🎁Contribute at least 6%. No-brainer.

ASML matches 100% of your first 3% and 50% of the next 3%. Contributing 6% earns the maximum 4.5% match.

You put in 6%, ASML adds 4.5%. So you get 4.5 / 6 = 75% of your investment for free!

Even if you later withdraw early and pay the 10% penalty plus income tax, the match usually still leaves you ahead, and it is worth doing even if you may leave the US.

Caveats: while employed you generally cannot withdraw 401(k) money before age 59½ except for hardship or other plan-allowed events, so treat it as money you will leave in. The ASML match is immediately vested, so it is yours even if you leave soon. Early withdrawals are also taxed as ordinary income.

2🧾What counts as compensation

Almost everything on your paycheck: salary, holiday pay, sick leave, bonus (STI), and patent awards. The ESPP bonus premium also counts: it is paid out as income once you have held the purchased ESPP shares for one year.

Exact definitions are set by the plan document. Confirm edge cases in the Summary Plan Description on NetBenefits.

3🏃💨Hit your own limit early? The match keeps going

If you front-load contributions and reach the employee limit before December ($24,500 for 2026; about $25,500 projected for 2027), paycheck deductions stop. ASML keeps contributing the match for the rest of the year as long as your deferral percentage stays at 6% or more and you are still under the compensation cap from item 4. There is no year-end true-up; the match simply continues with each paycheck.

Don't change your deferral percentage after hitting the limit. Lowering it below 6% reduces the match for the remaining paychecks.

4🚧The IRS caps the compensation that can be matched

Only compensation up to the IRS limit (IRC 401(a)(17)) counts for the match. Earning more does not increase it.

YearCompensation limitMax 4.5% match
2027 (projected, IRS not yet final)$375,000$16,875

So the most match you can receive is roughly $16,875 a year, however much you earn above the limit.

💡 Hit the income limit? There may be a workaround, especially new in 2027. Talk to the author of this page to find out.

5🚪After-tax contributions and the mega backdoor Roth

If the plan allows, you can contribute after-tax money beyond the regular limit, then convert or roll it into Roth. The total for all 401(k) money (yours, ASML's match, and after-tax) is capped by IRC 415(c):

YearTotal annual additions limit
2026 (official)$72,000
2027 (projected)$75,000

Plan your after-tax amount so it stays under the cap, since it is unclear whether hitting the cap mid-year stops the employer match. Keep your after-tax contributions modest to be safe.

Requires that the plan allows after-tax contributions and in-plan Roth conversion or in-service rollover. Convert promptly to minimize taxable earnings.

6🎂Age 50+: catch-up contributions

Catch-up contributions sit in a separate bucket, outside the regular deferral limit and the total limit above.

  • 2026: $8,000 extra for age 50+; $11,250 for ages 60 to 63.
  • Starting 2026, if your prior-year FICA wages exceeded $150,000, catch-up contributions must be made as Roth (after-tax).

Informational only, not tax or investment advice. Rules and ASML plan details can change: check the plan documents on NetBenefits.com and consult a tax professional. 2026 limits come from IRS Notice 2025-67. 2027 figures are industry projections until the IRS publishes them.
