Transfer Stocks to Fidelity

EquatePlus trading tools can be limited and costly. Transfer your stocks to a brokerage like Fidelity to access advanced trading tools, lower your costs, and retain your cost basis. Follow these simple steps.

⚠️ Important Warning: If you transfer an ESPP lot within 1 year of its purchase date, you might not receive the 20% bonus for holding more than one year. Please verify your lot purchase dates before transferring.
1

🏦 Open a Fidelity Account

If you don't already have one, the first step is to open a Fidelity Brokerage account.

2

🔗 Add Fidelity to EquatePlus

Log in to your EquatePlus account and link your new Fidelity brokerage account.

  • Go to EquatePlus.com and log in.
  • Click on the User Icon (top right).
  • Navigate to Financial Details > Brokerage account.
  • Click on Add new brokerage account.

3

✈️ Initiate Transfer

Once the account is added, you can proceed to transfer your stock lots.

  • Go to Transact > Transfer from the main menu.
  • Select the specific lot of stock you wish to transfer.
  • Submit your request.
Settlement typically takes ~1 week
